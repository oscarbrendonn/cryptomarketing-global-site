Privacy Policy

This policy explains information associated with visits to cryptomarketing.global and project enquiries sent to Crypto Marketing Global. Contact: Oscar Brendon, using the Telegram or email links below.

Updated: 2026-10-28

Website visits and hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare. Serving and protecting the website can involve technical information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information and request times. Cloudflare processes hosting and security information under its own privacy policy.

This site has no account registration, checkout or contact form.

Google Ads measurement

We use Google Ads to measure page visits and clicks on our Telegram contact link so we can assess advertising performance. In the current setup, advertising and analytics cookie storage is denied, and ad personalization is disabled.

Google may receive limited technical measurement signals, such as page URLs, request times and browser or device information. These signals can be sent without advertising or analytics cookies; denied cookie storage does not mean that no data is transmitted.

The website measures clicks on the link that opens our Telegram contact. It does not read or collect your Telegram messages. Links to trading bots are not counted as contact conversions.

Your appearance preference

If you choose light or dark mode, the site stores that choice in your browser under cmp-appearance. This local storage setting allows your choice to remain consistent between pages.

You can remove the preference by clearing this website’s browser data. It is not sent through a contact form or used by this site as an advertising identifier.

The introductory animation also stores a viewed marker (cmp-intro-seen-v1) in session storage for the current browser tab. This marker is used by the presentation and is not an account password or contact message.

Telegram and email enquiries

If you contact us, your messages may contain your name or handle, contact address, project links and the details you choose to share. We use those details to reply, plan the project, coordinate agreed work and maintain the relevant project correspondence.

Telegram and email providers also process messages through their own systems and privacy policies. Please share only information needed for your enquiry.

External links and referrals

Links to platforms, trading tools and the Crypto Marketing Premium website open separate services. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them. Referral URLs may pass a referral identifier to the destination service.

Retention and your requests

Enquiry and project information is kept for as long as needed to handle the conversation, deliver agreed work and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Contact us to ask about your information or request correction or deletion. Include enough context to identify the relevant conversation; we may need to verify the request. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
