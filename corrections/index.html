Point to the claim, and it will be checked

Platform features, policies and availability can change. A specific correction report is the fastest way to separate an outdated detail from a genuine error.

Updated: 2026-10-28

What to report

Report a factual error, broken source, incorrect attribution, outdated platform rule, material translation problem or commercial relationship that should have been disclosed. A difference of opinion may be reviewed, but it is not automatically a factual correction.

How to send a useful report

Send the page URL, quote the exact sentence, explain what appears wrong and include a primary source or other evidence when possible. Use @oscarbrencyrpto on Telegram or email oscarbrendonn@gmail.com.

How a report is reviewed

The claim should be checked against the closest reliable source. If it is inaccurate, the source content should be corrected first, along with metadata or structured data affected by the same claim. If the available evidence does not support a change, the original wording may remain.

Material corrections

A correction that changes the meaning of a page, a risk statement or a reader's understanding of a commercial relationship should be made clearly. Minor spelling, grammar or formatting fixes may be applied without a separate correction note.

Localized corrections

English is the source edition. When an error affects translated pages, the corrected meaning should be carried into each published locale and the related metadata where needed.
