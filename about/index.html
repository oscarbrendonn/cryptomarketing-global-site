About Crypto Marketing Global

Crypto Marketing Global helps crypto project teams plan marketing, community, content and technical work. Explore service details in your language and discuss your project directly with Oscar Brendon.

Updated: 2026-10-28

Services built around your project

The catalogue covers campaign planning, community management, content and design, PR, platform visibility, technical tools and exchange listing support. Service pages explain the available options, the information needed to begin and the work involved.

Exchange listing support includes preparing project information, coordinating application materials and following up on submissions. Each request is planned for the selected exchange and listing route.

From enquiry to delivery

Start with your project website, target audience, preferred platforms and campaign goal. We review the brief together and confirm the deliverables, schedule in writing before starting.

Campaign coordination brings the selected services into one plan. Delivery materials, agreed reports and follow-up work are defined in that plan so your team knows what to provide and what it will receive.
