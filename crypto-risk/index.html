Crypto Tools and Risk Information

Crypto Marketing Global provides marketing, design, content, community and technical support. This page explains the separate considerations involved when you choose to use the third-party crypto tools linked from our catalogue.

Updated: 2026-10-28

Marketing services and trading decisions

Our service catalogue describes work for project teams. Buying a marketing service does not buy a token, open a trading account or authorize us to trade on your behalf.

Trading-tool guides explain provider features and setup steps. They are general information, not personalized investment advice. Your choice of a token, provider, transaction or trading strategy remains your own.

Prices, liquidity and transaction costs

Cryptoasset prices can move rapidly, and a position can lose some or all of its value. Low liquidity and slippage can cause the execution price to differ from a displayed quote.

Before confirming a transaction, check the network, token contract, total amount, provider fees and network fees. Automated orders and trading bots also need review and monitoring; a software setting is not a trading outcome.

Wallet and account security

Create or connect a wallet only through the provider’s official app or website. Verify the domain, permissions, network and recipient address before connecting a wallet or transferring assets.

Keep recovery phrases and private keys private. Crypto Marketing Global does not request these credentials through Telegram, email or this website. Access to trading accounts, wallets and assets stays with you and the provider you choose.

Protect accounts, keys, data, and publishing authority

Use the least access required for the task. Prefer role-based platform access, separate operator accounts, time-limited permissions, and auditable approvals. Never share a seed phrase, private key, password, two-factor code, unrestricted API key, or custody authority for a marketing task. Define who can publish, spend, export data, install a bot, change a destination, or remove another operator before access is granted.

The agreement should cover data sources, retention, deletion, incident escalation, subcontractors, and access removal at the end of the work. Keep owned domains, analytics properties, ad accounts, communities, creative files, and reporting exports under the project's control. A clean handover is part of delivery, not an optional favor after the relationship ends.

Third-party platforms and availability

The trading tools and platforms linked here operate separately from this website. Their current terms, supported networks, regional availability, fees and security arrangements apply when you use them.
