Detailed service scope

What are token verifications and updates?

Token Verifications & Updates brings together services for maintaining your project information across crypto platforms. It covers wallet branding, verification marks, warning removal and the company or brand details behind your project.

Choose the option that matches the platform and task you need: a logo update, token listing, profile badge, warning removal or company service. The catalog separates these services so each order addresses a specific project need.
