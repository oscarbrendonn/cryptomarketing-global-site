Detailed service scope

How are approved engagement actions coordinated across X and Telegram?

Connect X posts with your Telegram campaign

This workflow brings X campaign links into the Telegram group used by your project. Moderators can collect submitted posts, review the message and choose when to share it with the community. Campaign links and publication tasks stay together in one manageable queue.

Connected accounts, Telegram groups and moderator actions are configured for the campaign requirements. The panel can display submitted links, scheduled messages and completed steps. Moderators control publishing, while activity records identify the posts reviewed or shared with the community.

Development and Service scope

The selected X accounts, Telegram groups and moderator workflow define the development requirements. Available features include a review queue, scheduled messages and a management panel.

A sample campaign post and the Telegram group requirements support the assessment. The proposal specifies workflow development, installation and support.

Inputs, Delivery and Handoff

Testing demonstrates link intake, permission checks, rejected inputs, rate-limit handling and the approved action path with designated accounts. Handoff includes configuration and a disable procedure. Logs show workflow events only; they are not described as organic sentiment or independent community response.

X and Telegram Engagement Workflow starts only after scope, timing and availability match the written brief. Before we start, we write down what we will do, which access we need and for how long. At the end you receive a short handover: what was done, the approved files, and anything that depends on a third party. You also know who is responsible, what proof you will get and when we check in with you.
