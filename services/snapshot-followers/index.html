Detailed service scope

What is Snapshot?

What Snapshot Followers Campaign Support Covers

Snapshot provides off-chain governance spaces where communities can publish proposals and voting strategies. Followers are a visible relationship to a space, but they are not voting power. This service covers only the follower item and does not configure strategies, delegate wallets or influence proposal outcomes.

Keep follower delivery separate from voting power, wallet eligibility, proposal participation and governance outcomes. For Snapshot Followers Campaign Support, the signed-off brief names the controllable deliverable and the destination separately. That keeps a service label from being mistaken for Snapshot approval, an algorithm claim or a forecast about user behavior.

Scope for Snapshot

Provide the Snapshot space URL, network context where relevant, current follower display, requested quantity and timing. Space setup, proposal writing and voting-strategy work are separate technical tasks and are not inferred from a follower order.

Snapshot follower delivery for a confirmed space URL, quantity and delivery window. Snapshot Followers Campaign Support includes discovery, scope definition, audience and message planning, approved content preparation, channel coordination, publication records, attribution setup, and an evidence-led summary.

Inputs, Delivery and Handoff

A completion record points to the exact Snapshot space and states the requested unit and observed state. It does not list followers as eligible voters or token holders. Proposal participation and voting power require their own Snapshot or on-chain evidence and are excluded from follower reporting.

Snapshot Followers Campaign Support starts only after scope, timing and availability match the written brief. Before we start, we write down what we will do, which access we need and for how long. At the end you receive a short handover: what was done, the approved files, and anything that depends on a third party. You also know who is responsible, what proof you will get and when we check in with you.
