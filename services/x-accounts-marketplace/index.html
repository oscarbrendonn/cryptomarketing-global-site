How does a marketplace for established X accounts work?

What Aged X / Twitter Accounts Marketplace Covers

The Aged X Accounts Marketplace page concerns account availability and transfer support, subject to current X rules. Account age is a historical attribute, not proof of authenticity, reputation or future access. Each proposed account must have its provenance, present status and permitted use reviewed before a sale.

Confirm account provenance, current status and permitted transfer use, while recognizing that X rules and enforcement still apply after delivery. For Aged X / Twitter Accounts Marketplace, the signed-off brief names the controllable deliverable and the destination separately. That keeps a service label from being mistaken for X approval, an algorithm claim or a forecast about user behavior.

Scope for X

The request identifies quantity, intended legitimate use, market, profile requirements and handoff needs. The seller must disclose known restrictions and current access context. No account is represented as ban-proof or suitable for deceptive identity use.

Inputs, Delivery and Handoff

Handoff records the specific account, current profile state, agreed credentials-transfer process and client acknowledgment of platform risk. Recovery and security settings are changed through a controlled process where permitted.

Aged X / Twitter Accounts Marketplace starts only after scope, timing and availability match the written brief. Before we start, we write down what we will do, which access we need and for how long. At the end you receive a short handover: what was done, the approved files, and anything that depends on a third party. You also know who is responsible, what proof you will get and when we check in with you.
