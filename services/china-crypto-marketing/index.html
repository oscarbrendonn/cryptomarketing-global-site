Detailed service scope

How can a crypto project be positioned and promoted professionally in the Chinese market?

Define the Chinese-speaking audience, market and operating boundary

The engagement begins by separating language from geography. A project may need to reach audiences in Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, other regional hubs or Chinese-speaking communities elsewhere. We document the intended audience, product stage, supported jurisdictions, approved claims, campaign objective and the action visitors should be able to complete before choosing a channel or producing content.

The written brief records which markets are in scope, which activities require client legal approval, who owns product and token facts, and which platform rules apply. Mainland activation is never assumed from a Chinese translation alone. If a requested placement, financial promotion or product action cannot be verified for the intended audience, it remains outside delivery until the client supplies suitable legal and platform confirmation.

Localize the project narrative instead of translating it word for word

Chinese-language content starts with the terms the product and its audience genuinely use. We adapt the value proposition, product explanation, token utility, onboarding steps, frequently asked questions, campaign calls to action and evidence hierarchy while preserving the approved meaning. Names, contract addresses, quantities, dates and risk statements remain tied to client-approved records.

A multilingual content map keeps the Chinese version connected to the correct source pages, documentation and language alternatives. Navigation, metadata and internal links are prepared for the actual site architecture rather than copied mechanically from English. Review stages distinguish linguistic quality, technical accuracy and legal approval so one sign-off is not expected to cover three different responsibilities.

Choose verified discovery surfaces for the campaign objective

MoonTok can be evaluated as a Chinese-language token discovery and advertising surface. The exact token page, chain, current placement, timing, language and order flow are checked before a campaign is scoped. A trending or advertising option is treated as a platform-controlled format, not as proof of lasting rank, broad national reach or endorsement.

Ave.ai can support Chinese-language on-chain discovery, community routes and reviewed ecosystem-partner activity. Its role is defined separately from MoonTok because the platforms have different interfaces, requirements and evidence. The channel plan may combine these verified surfaces with project-owned pages and approved community channels, but each deliverable keeps its own destination, owner and completion test.

Build a consistent campaign from first impression to qualified action

Campaign production can include Chinese-language landing pages, platform descriptions, launch announcements, visual briefs, educational posts, partner materials, community response guidance and frequently asked questions. Every asset is connected to a documented project fact and a clear next step so the campaign does not rely on vague excitement or disconnected graphics.

The distribution plan defines what is published by the project, what may be submitted to a platform or partner, who approves the final version and how questions return to the responsible team. If custom software, localized forms, analytics or content management is needed, it is scoped as a technical deliverable with its own permissions, testing and handover rather than hidden inside a generic marketing promise.

Measure useful response and improve the next campaign cycle

Measurement begins with decisions the team can act on. Depending on the approved scope, reporting can cover qualified visits, documentation use, community transitions, enquiries, completed forms, partner activity and observable placement records. Platform screenshots show a particular delivery state; they do not prove future visibility, retention or audience quality.

Reviews compare message clarity, landing-page behaviour and audience questions, then recommend the next content, channel or product-explanation change. Metrics are interpreted within the recorded sample and never converted into promises about holders, trading volume, token price or financial return.

Keep approvals, platform dependencies and handover explicit

The delivery record lists approved copy, source materials, platform destinations, campaign windows, owners, access roles and acceptance evidence. MoonTok and Ave.ai retain control of their interfaces, inventory, eligibility and review decisions. A current platform option can change, so the final order uses the verified live format rather than preserving an outdated label.

Handover identifies client-owned accounts, editable files, reports, unresolved dependencies and the next review date. Ongoing community work, content production, new markets, software features and campaign monitoring are agreed separately.
