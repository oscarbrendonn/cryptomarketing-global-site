How are market-price updates delivered in Telegram?

What Telegram Price Bot Covers

A Telegram Price Bot retrieves market data for an agreed token or pair and exposes it through commands or scheduled messages. It is an information interface, not a price oracle for trading or settlement. Source selection matters because different pools, exchanges and aggregation methods can report different values.

Name the market-data source, pair selection, refresh interval, fallback behavior and command permissions before deployment. For Telegram Price Bot, the signed-off brief names the controllable deliverable and the destination separately. That keeps a service label from being mistaken for Telegram approval, an algorithm claim or a forecast about user behavior.

Inputs, Delivery and Handoff

Testing compares bot output with the selected source at recorded times, checks decimal handling and demonstrates stale-data or outage messages. Handoff includes configuration, permissions and deployment notes. A match confirms the integration at that moment.

Telegram Price Bot starts only after scope, timing and availability match the written brief. Before we start, we write down what we will do, which access we need and for how long. At the end you receive a short handover: what was done, the approved files, and anything that depends on a third party. You also know who is responsible, what proof you will get and when we check in with you.
