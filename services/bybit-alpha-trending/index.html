Detailed service scope

Momentum inside the Bybit app

Millions of people open the Bybit app every day, and Bybit Alpha is where many of them look for early-stage tokens. Appearing in that section at the right moment puts your project in front of traders who are already in a buying mood.

The first days after a listing are usually the most valuable. A trending campaign scheduled for exactly that period keeps the token visible while your community is sharing the news.

Campaign steps

We begin by confirming your Bybit Alpha token page, the contract and the official links. Next we fix the campaign window together and coordinate the trending activity across it. Throughout the campaign you receive brief updates on where the token appears.

If the token is not on Bybit Alpha yet, we handle the listing first and line the campaign up behind it, so there is no gap between the listing and the visibility push.

Information to send

We need the Bybit Alpha token page link, the ticker, the network and the contract address, plus your website and social accounts. Share the campaign dates you prefer as well.

Planned announcements, partnerships or exchange news help us place the campaign on the right day. The better the timing, the more your community can do with the attention.
