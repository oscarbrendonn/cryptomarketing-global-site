Detailed service scope

What is DEX Screener?

DEX Screener is a market data platform for tokens traded on decentralized exchanges. It turns blockchain activity into price charts, trading-pair pages and live metrics such as volume, liquidity and recent transactions across supported networks.

Users can search for a token by name or contract address, inspect the pools where it trades and compare activity over different periods. Watchlists, price alerts and views for new pairs and market movers help them follow tokens from one place.

What is DEX Screener Trending?

DEX Screener marketing puts your trading pair at the centre of the campaign. We run trending promotion and offer Votes and Watch packages, so the token can build a stronger presence where traders research charts and new pairs. Each service can be booked individually or combined in the same campaign.

For DEX Screener campaigns, we configure scripts for your pair link and chain, then schedule the chosen Trending, Votes or Watch services around the promotion period.

Scope for DEX Screener

Provide the DEX Screener pair link, network, pair address, current page state, requested vote unit and campaign window. A token with multiple pools must specify the intended pair because completion cannot be transferred between routes silently.
