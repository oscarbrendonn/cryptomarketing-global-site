Detailed service scope

What is CoinCatapult?

Prepare your CoinCatapult project page

Build the CoinCatapult submission around a consistent token identity: name, symbol, network, contract and official links.

For a page already online, send its exact address and choose the existing votes package. The order identifies that destination and requested quantity, making campaign follow-up straightforward.

Scope for CoinCatapult

Share your project details and preferred delivery window so we can confirm the scope and availability before payment.

Choose votes separately for the published page.

Send the CoinCatapult coin URL, contract address, chain, present counter, requested number of votes and delivery window. A broken, replaced or ineligible listing pauses the request because votes cannot be redirected without approval.

Inputs, Delivery and Handoff

Choose votes separately for the published page. For listing work, send the token name, symbol, network, contract address, description and official links. For votes, include the published project URL and requested quantity.

The completion record references the same listing URL used at intake and states the agreed unit and time window. Where the counter remains public, before-and-after context may be captured. That evidence covers the requested listing activity only; it does not establish visitor identity, retention or downstream trading behavior.

CoinCatapult Votes Support starts only after scope, timing and availability match the written brief. Before we start, we write down what we will do, which access we need and for how long. At the end you receive a short handover: what was done, the approved files, and anything that depends on a third party. You also know who is responsible, what proof you will get and when we check in with you.
