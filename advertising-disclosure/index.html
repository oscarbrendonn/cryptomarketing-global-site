Know when content has a commercial purpose

Crypto Marketing Global sells marketing services. Service pages are commercial content, and readers should be able to distinguish them from independent educational material.

Updated: 2026-10-28

Our service pages

Pages that describe or invite an inquiry for a Crypto Marketing Global service are promotions for that service. Calls to action may lead to the primary Telegram account or the public email address.

Sponsored or paid content

If a third party pays for, supplies or materially influences a page, post or placement, the content should carry a clear sponsorship or advertising label near the relevant material. Payment must not be presented as independent editorial approval.

Affiliate links

If a link can generate a commission or other benefit, that relationship should be disclosed before or beside the link. A disclosure should name the nature of the relationship in plain language rather than rely on an unclear symbol.

Platform names do not imply endorsement

References to exchanges, wallets, networks, listing sites or social platforms identify the subject of a service or article. Crypto Marketing Global is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by a named third party unless a page expressly states the relationship and supports it with evidence.

Commercial relationships do not change factual standards

Errors in sponsored or commercial content can be reported through the same corrections process used for other pages.
